# Code of conduct

git-shuttle follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be kind and patient. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and people who disagree with you; assume good faith.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or publishing someone's private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic, and say plainly what you need.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block someone who keeps breaking them.

## Reporting

If something happened to you or you saw it, write to the maintainer privately through the contact on
[adityaoryza.my.id](https://adityaoryza.my.id/en), or open a private report from the **Security** tab of the repository
([report](https://github.com/adityaoryza/git-shuttle/security/advisories/new)) and say it is about conduct. Reports are read within a week
and treated as confidential.
